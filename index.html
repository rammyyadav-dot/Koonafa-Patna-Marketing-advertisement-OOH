CLEAR ANSWERS.

What does Koonafa do?

Koonafa offers brand strategy, creative advertising, digital marketing and a Patna-focused out-of-home advertising planning service, including hoardings, signage and integrated campaigns.

What is OOH advertising?

Out-of-home (OOH) advertising includes billboards, hoardings, unipoles, transit media and digital displays seen outside the home. It helps brands reach people across physical locations.

Can Koonafa plan hoarding advertising in Patna?

Koonafa can discuss hoarding and outdoor campaign requirements in Patna. Specific locations, availability, costs and permissions must be confirmed during quotation.

How much does billboard advertising cost in Patna?

Costs vary by site, dimensions, display technology, campaign duration, production and approvals. Request a quotation for a confirmed site and campaign period.

Can outdoor and digital campaigns work together?

Yes. Outdoor advertising can build local awareness while digital ads can support audience targeting, website visits and measurable enquiries.

How do I request a campaign quotation?

Send your business category, advertising goal, preferred locations, campaign dates and approximate budget to hello@koonafa.com or WhatsApp +91 91727 12982.

Do you plan LED, unipole, gantry and highway hoardings in Bihar?

Yes. LED billboard advertising in Patna, unipole locations, gantry advertising, highway hoardings and footover-bridge panels can all be discussed. Availability, illumination and permits are confirmed before a booking.

Can you plan bus shelter and transit advertising in Patna?

Bus shelter advertising, BQS media, and transit ads around junctions such as Dak Bungla Chauraha are part of outdoor media planning. Each format depends on the operator and the site.

Do you handle flex printing and illuminated signage in Patna?

Large-format flex printing and illuminated signage boards can be planned with the hoarding or shopfront. Production is quoted against the approved size and the material the site allows.

Can outdoor ads and local SEO run as one Patna campaign?

They should. Outdoor branding combined with local SEO, performance marketing, social media and geotargeted ads keeps the street message and the search result aligned for local businesses.

Which Patna corridors can a media plan cover?

Briefs often name Boring Road, Bailey Road, Kankarbagh, Exhibition Road, Fraser Road, Patna Junction, Saguna More, Danapur, Patliputra Colony, Rajendra Nagar, Airport Road, the AIIMS Patna road, Mahavir Mandir and Gandhi Maidan. These are request areas, not a stock list.
